Sources: Montana Fish, Wildlife & Parks harvest estimates (myfwp.mt.gov), the Deer, Elk & Antelope regulations booklet, and FWP's black bear quota status page. FWP counts deer hunters per district, not per species, so for deer "per hunter" means animals of that species per deer hunter. Hunter numbers are surveyed every other year (deer in odd years, elk and antelope in even years); other years show harvest only. Antelope districts have their own numbering.
